- **Abstract:** Deuteration is a powerful metric to gain insight into the formation pathways of interstellar molecules in star forming regions. Our primary goal is to search for the fully deuterated form of methyl cyanide, CD3CN, in the ISM. We used a sensitive imaging spectral survey of the protostellar system L1551 IRS5 in the Taurus molecular cloud performed as part of the Large Program carried out with the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) called Complex Organic Molecules in Protostars with ALMA Spectral Surveys (COMPASS). Multiply deuterated isotopologs are crucial to understand the formation and inheritance of molecules in the ISM by coupling observations and astrochemical models. Unfortunately, no model has so far investigated the deuteration of methyl cyanide up to the fully deuterated form. [Abridged]

- **Abstract:** Only five out of the $\sim$350 molecules detected in the interstellar medium (ISM) carry the isocyanate functional group (NCO), the essential substructure of peptide bonds in proteins. Previous studies have predicted that the O-protonated isomer HNCOH$^+$ should be present in the ISM; yet this species has remained elusive. - **PDF:** [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40049.pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40049.pdf)  
- **Abstract:** eROSITA bubbles (eRBs) in our Galaxy are thought to be due to the energy injection by the central star-forming region or the central Super Massive Black Hole. Identifying the origin of these bubbles is crucial in constraining the origin of the Fermi Bubbles and the nature of the Galactic feedback. However, some recent opinions suggest that the eRBs may not even have been produced by any central source, but rather a result of a star-formation ring at $R\sim 5$ kpc. - **Abstract:** Circumgalactic Medium (CGM) plays a critical role in galactic evolution, serving as the primary reservoir for accretion and outflows. In this work, we present a novel statistical analysis to derive CGM metallicity from the dispersion measure (DM) of 25 Fast Radio Burst (FRB) sightlines. By combining metallicity-dependent oxygen absorption DM estimates with metallicity-independent FRB DMs, we directly constrain the metallicity of the hot phase of the Milky Way (MW) CGM. - **Abstract:** (abridged) Stellar feedback regulates galactic baryon cycles, but galaxy-scale simulations often cannot resolve supernova cooling radii. When the cooling radius is resolved, all supernova energy is injected into the host cell as thermal energy; otherwise, the host cell is refined and terminal momentum is deposited into surrounding gas cells.
